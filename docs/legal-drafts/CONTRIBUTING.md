## Section - Statements & Agreements (for contributing)

Because this is a collaborative worldbuilding project, you must understand and aware that:

1. The Lead Maintainer serves only as a custodian and curator, 
   and Manager are to help the Lead Maintainer:
   * a. To organize and present contributions in a unified, accessible form.
   * b. To ensure consistency and clarity of attribution.
   * c. To moderate against hate speech, harassment, or calls for violence

2. By contributing to the Project:
   * a. You allow others to see, use, adapt, and build upon your submitted Content.
   * b. You agree to publish Your submitted Software Utility under [...] license
   * c. You grant The Project a non-exclusive, perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free, worldwide permission to integrate, adapt, and publish Your Contribution as part of the Project.
   * d. You retain author attribution for your specific contribution.

3. Therefore, it is VERY RECOMMENDED that you clearly sign on or, even, in 
   your Depictive Works to prevent thefts
